Frederick CountyPILOT COUNTY

Last updated 2026-09-01 · Tracker run 3 (monthly)
Active — Under Construction Application Pause thru 12/31/26 Pause End Condition Now Uncertain

Quantum Frederick — a 2.4GW facility — remains under construction, unaffected by the pause. The pause on new applications runs through Dec 31, 2026, but its own trigger condition — the state's impact report — has now missed its statutory deadline with no new completion date set. Two public information sessions are scheduled for early September.

Governance StatusVerified

Confirmed active
Quantum Frederick — 2.4GW, currently under construction. Not affected by the application pause.
Through Dec 31, 2026
Application pause on new data center applications remains in effect. Does not apply to properties with approvals predating the Council's consideration of CB 25-09 (Jun 17, 2025), and does not stop construction already underway.
Effective statewide, Jul 1, 2026
PSC large-load registry requirement (Utility RELIEF Act) — confirmed still active for Frederick as of this run.
Missed Sept 1, 2026
State Data Center Impact Analysis confirmed delayed. Sen. Karen Lewis Young (D-Frederick), the bill's sponsor, confirms the report will not meet its statutory deadline; no new completion date has been set. The pause's own stated end condition — "until December 31, after the State issues its Data Center Impact Analysis and Report" — is now unresolved. The county has not yet said whether the Dec 31 date holds regardless.
Sept 2 & Sept 9, 2026
Two informational open houses on county data center regulation (see Calendar). Informational only — no formal public comment or testimony taken at either session.

Jurisdiction NoteVerified

Search results have repeatedly surfaced a data center / transmission fight involving a "Frederick County" Board of Supervisors, a Valley North transmission line, and joint meetings with the City of Winchester and Clarke County. That is Frederick County, Virginia — a different jurisdiction entirely, excluded from this page. The name collision is a recurring risk specific to this county and worth double-checking on every future search.
