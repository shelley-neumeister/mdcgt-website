Montgomery CountyPILOT COUNTY

Last updated 2026-08-08 · Tracker run 1
Active Proposal 18-Month Moratorium

Atmosphere Data Centers proposes the Dickerson Data Center Campus — five buildings, 1.1M sq ft, 300MW, on 700 acres of the county's agricultural reserve, co-located on a coal plant site decommissioned in 2020. After a 6-month executive-order pause was formally appealed by the developer, the County Council voted unanimously (11–0) to replace it with a legislative 18-month moratorium — a stronger, harder-to-challenge mechanism that the Council President says closes off the appeal route entirely.

Governance TimelineVerified

Jan 2026
County Executive Elrich releases legislative/policy recommendations, drawing partly on Frederick's and Prince George's prior reports.
Feb 2026
Permitting gets underway for the Dickerson site.
Jun 12, 2026
Elrich signs a 6-month moratorium executive order — explicitly covering Dickerson, unlike Council member Glass's earlier proposal, which had exempted that site.
Early Jul 2026
Atmosphere Data Centers formally appeals Elrich's executive order, arguing he exceeded his authority.
Jul 28, 2026
County Council votes unanimously (11–0) to pass Expedited Bill 19-26 — an 18-month moratorium, supplanting the executive order. A compromise between a 6-month and a 2-year proposal. Having gone through full legislative process with public hearings, Council President Fani-González states it's harder to legally challenge than the executive order was.

Scope & Escape ClauseVerified

No escape clause identified — this is a fixed 18-month term, not built around a triggering replacement law the way Prince George's is. Worth confirming directly with county code before relying on this.

Scope gap: advocates note data centers under 25 MW are not covered by the moratorium — an open lane for smaller facilities, worth treating the same way as a site-shaped-legislation flag.
