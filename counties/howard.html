Howard CountyNOT A PILOT COUNTY

Last updated 2026-09-01 · Tracker run 1 (first full profile)
17-Month Moratorium — Enacted

The S.M.A.R.T. (Strategic Moratorium for Assessing Responsible Technology) Siting Act — Council Bill 31-2026 — passed unanimously on June 1, 2026, pausing new data center development in Howard County through November 2, 2027. Unlike the counties driven by a single controversial proposal, this one is explicitly framed as getting ahead of the issue: no named project triggered it, and a task force is now being staffed to write the rules before any project arrives.

Governance TimelineVerified

May 18, 2026
Public hearing on CB31, introduced by Councilmember Christiana Rigby. Several residents, including high school students, testify about harms observed in Northern Virginia's data center buildout.
Jun 1, 2026
Council unanimously approves CB31-2026, the S.M.A.R.T. Siting Act — a 17-month moratorium on new data center development, through Nov. 2, 2027. Creates a task force to study best practices for siting and location and recommend future regulations.
Jul 2026
County opens applications for the SMART Data Center Task Force.

Scope & ExemptionsVerified

Verified
The Act does not apply to state or local government entities, Howard Community College, or the Howard County Library System.
Verified
Task force is charged with issuing a final report recommending best practices for data center siting and location, plus development standards, before the moratorium lifts.
Framing
Quote from bill proponents: "Passing CB31 does not mean we are anti-technology, it means we are pro-responsibility." CCAN Action Fund's Mike Tidwell called it "a line in the sand."
