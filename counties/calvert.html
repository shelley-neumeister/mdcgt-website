Calvert CountyNOT A PILOT COUNTY

Last updated 2026-09-01
Active Proposal 6-Month Moratorium Enacted

Natelli Holdings' "Appeal Digital Park" remains a pending proposal. The county's Aug 18, 2026 hearing did not just consider a pause — it enacted one: the Board of County Commissioners voted unanimously for a six-month moratorium on data center site plan approvals, effective Aug 28, 2026. This page had not been checked since that hearing; the status shown previously ("pause not yet enacted") was two weeks stale.

◆ Material change since 2026-08-05 — moratorium enacted, chip status flipped.

Board of County Commissioners holds a public hearing — hearing room packed to standing room only, more than 30 speakers — and votes unanimously to enact a six-month moratorium on data center site plan approvals. County will not accept new site plan applications during the moratorium period.
Aug 28, 2026
Moratorium becomes effective, per Title 4 of the Land Use Article of the Maryland Annotated Code.
Future meeting, undated
Commissioners expected to discuss potential changes to the Calvert County Zoning Ordinance regarding data centers as a permitted use. No date set yet.

Political ContextAssessment

This moratorium follows directly from the June 2026 primary, in which three incumbent commissioners who opposed a data center moratorium were defeated. The proposal had originally gained support from county commissioners after residents first learned of it in February; by August, the political composition of the board had shifted enough to pass a pause unanimously. Resident sentiment reported at the hearing leaned toward wanting the moratorium "stronger," not weaker — worth watching whether that pressure shapes the zoning ordinance discussion still to come.

Regional ContextAssessment

Calvert sits within the Southern Maryland cluster of counties (alongside Charles and St. Mary's) facing developer pressure tied to proximity to power infrastructure — in this region, primarily Calvert Cliffs nuclear plant. This page still carries less source depth than the four pilot-county pages; treat entries here as a working record rather than a full baseline.
