Charles CountyPILOT COUNTY

Last updated 2026-09-01 · Tracker run 2
Active Proposal Contested — No Formal Pause Zoning Currently Blocks Data Centers

TeraWulf Inc. proposes the Chesapeake Data Campus at the former Morgantown Generating Station on the Potomac — pairing a 500MW–1GW data center buildout with a plan to convert retired coal units to natural gas. Charles starts from a default of blocked: data centers are not currently a permitted use anywhere in the county's zoning code. Status this run is unchanged — the Board of Commissioners' final vote is still pending — but reporting has firmed up one adjacent fact worth carrying forward: St. Mary's County confirms directly, on the record, that it has zero landed proposals.

Governance TimelineVerified

2022
Morgantown's coal-fired units retired five years ahead of schedule (GenOn, prior owner), citing environmental compliance costs. Oil-fired units remain operational. The site was found illegally discharging coal ash into the Potomac as late as 2019 — the area is a designated overburdened community.
Feb 2026
TeraWulf announces the Morgantown acquisition.
Mar 2026
Charles County Planning Commission (advisory) recommends denying the zoning text amendment (ZTA) in its original form, suggesting tighter restrictions: limit to industrial zones, ban use of drinking-water sources for cooling, require the company to pay its own infrastructure costs.
Apr 2026
Board of County Commissioners votes 3–2 to send the ZTA back for revision rather than act on it directly.
Jun 1, 2026
Revised ZTA (narrowed to industrial zones) draws a second unanimous Planning Commission denial recommendation, after nearly four hours of public testimony and 50+ speakers.
Jul 28, 2026
FERC approves TeraWulf's purchase of the Morgantown plant — a federal, ownership-level approval, explicitly separate from county zoning authority. The county says it plays no formal role in approving the ownership transfer itself.
Status: still pending (confirmed as of 9/1/26)
The Board of County Commissioners' final vote on the ZTA is still not located in current sources. This remains the single most important open item on this page — unchanged over the past month.
