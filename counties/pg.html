Prince George's CountyPILOT COUNTY

Last updated 2026-09-01 · Tracker run 4
Active — Multiple Proposals 2-Year Moratorium — Longest In State

Prince George's holds the longest moratorium of any Maryland jurisdiction — two years, adopted Jul 7, 2026 — while three separate proposals move through the pipeline: Landover Mall/Brightseat, Konterra (Laurel), and a Brandywine study area. LDR-155 remains the presumptive off-ramp vehicle with no hearing date located. New this run: on-record positioning from Council leadership suggests the politics around any future legislation may be less accommodating than the moratorium alone implies.

◆ No change to the moratorium or LDR-155 status — but new political context this run.

Governance StatusVerified

The two-year moratorium (adopted Jul 7, 2026) remains in effect — the longest of any pause or ban currently on record statewide. LDR-155 has no new hearing date located and is still the presumptive off-ramp vehicle out of the moratorium. Executive Order No. 1-2026 formally supports forthcoming Task Force recommendations.

Council Leadership PositioningNew This Run

Verified — Direct Quote
Council Chair Krystal Oriadha, who sponsored the two-year moratorium, has stated on record that she personally supports a full ban and considers the two-year pause a compromise, not her actual position: "I support a full ban. That's what I've always supported." Relevant to how she is likely to engage with any by-right carve-out legislation, including LDR-155.
The Banner, 7/8/26; Inside Climate News, 7/10/26
Verified
Council member Jolene Ivey, who helped pass the county's 2020 data-center tax exemptions and initially favored fast-track approval of the Landover proposal, has shifted to supporting "strict guidelines" and now wants to repeal those same tax incentives. A genuine reversal among sitting Council leadership, not just rhetoric.
The Banner, 6/17/26 & 6/21/26
