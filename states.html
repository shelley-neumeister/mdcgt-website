Prince George's County, Maryland

Holds Maryland's longest data center moratorium (two years, adopted Jul 7, 2026) while three proposals sit in the pipeline: Landover Mall/Brightseat, Konterra (Laurel), and a Brandywine study area. LDR-155 is the presumptive off-ramp, and no hearing date has been set.
