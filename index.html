Cross-Cutting Pattern — Assessment

Every documented proposal or pause sits on the I‑270/I‑95/Baltimore–Washington corridor or the Southern Maryland peninsula near power infrastructure — Calvert Cliffs nuclear, the former Morgantown and Chalk Point fossil sites.

The entire Eastern Shore (aside from Queen Anne's pause, with no named project) and all of Western Maryland show no hosting activity — yet Western Maryland carries new transmission infrastructure (the Mid-Atlantic Resiliency Link) built to serve demand elsewhere. Infrastructure burden without hosting benefit, concentrated in the counties with the least documented say in the matter.

How this board is built

"No documented activity found" means no proposal, pause, or ban surfaced in our current public search — it is not a confirmed absence. We treat the quiet list as lower-confidence than the active list, which is source-cited throughout.
