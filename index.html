Cross-Cutting Pattern — Assessment

Every documented proposal or pause sits on the I‑270/I‑95/Baltimore–Washington corridor or the Southern Maryland peninsula near power infrastructure — Calvert Cliffs nuclear, the former Morgantown and Chalk Point fossil sites. Washington County's newly-corrected pause is the first exception to this pattern found so far — worth a closer look once its full profile is built.

The Eastern Shore and most of Western Maryland still show no hosting activity — yet Western Maryland carries new transmission infrastructure (the Mid-Atlantic Resiliency Link) built to serve demand elsewhere. Infrastructure burden without hosting benefit, concentrated in the counties with the least documented say in the matter.

How this board is built

"No documented activity found" means no proposal, pause, or ban surfaced in our current public search — it is not a confirmed absence. Washington County's correction this update is a live example of why: a gap in search results is a gap in our search, not necessarily a gap in reality. In the other direction: St. Mary's County was previously counted as "active" on the strength of commissioner opposition alone. On review, opposition to a proposal that doesn't exist isn't documented activity by our own standard — its count was corrected down, even though its card still carries real, useful information.
