Cross-Cutting Pattern — Assessment

Revised this update. Last month's read — that only the I‑270/I‑95 corridor and Southern Maryland were live, with the Eastern Shore quiet — does not hold anymore. This update's broad sweep found that five of the Eastern Shore's nine jurisdictions have taken governance action (Queen Anne's, Kent, Caroline, Wicomico, Worcester), and a sixth (Dorchester) is actively considering it. Most of these are preemptive — no named proposal — but Caroline County's Federalsburg site is a live, real proposal facing organized local opposition, not a hypothetical one.

Western Maryland still shows no hosting activity in Garrett or Allegany, but both carry the Mid-Atlantic Resiliency Link transmission line built to serve demand elsewhere — infrastructure burden without hosting benefit, concentrated in the counties with the least documented say in the matter. That pattern, unlike the Eastern Shore's, has held.

How this board is built

"No documented activity found" means no proposal, pause, or ban surfaced in our current public search — it is not a confirmed absence. This update is the clearest example yet of why: a direct sweep of the 13 jurisdictions that previously had no individual profile turned up enacted governance action in nine of them — most of it never surfacing in earlier statewide searches. In the other direction: St. Mary's County remains correctly excluded from the activity count, since opposition to a proposal that doesn't exist isn't documented activity by our own standard.
